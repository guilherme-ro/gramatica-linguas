# English Grammar Cheat Sheet

A quick reference guide to the essential rules of English grammar.

---

## 1. Parts of Speech

| Part of Speech | Function | Examples |
|----------------|----------|----------|
| **Noun** | Person, place, thing, idea | book, city, happiness, teacher |
| **Pronoun** | Replaces a noun | I, you, he, she, it, we, they, this, that |
| **Verb** | Action or state of being | run, is, have, think, become |
| **Adjective** | Describes a noun | big, beautiful, quick, red |
| **Adverb** | Describes a verb, adjective, or other adverb | quickly, very, well, often |
| **Preposition** | Shows relationship | in, on, at, by, with, for, to |
| **Conjunction** | Connects words/clauses | and, but, or, because, although |
| **Interjection** | Expresses emotion | Oh!, Wow!, Ouch! |

---

## 2. Articles

| Article | Use | Examples |
|---------|-----|----------|
| **a / an** | Indefinite (singular, non-specific) | a book, an apple |
| **the** | Definite (specific or unique) | the sun, the book I read |
| **Ø (zero)** | Plural or uncountable (general) | Books are useful. Water is essential. |

**Rules:**
- Use **a** before consonant sounds: *a university, a European*
- Use **an** before vowel sounds: *an hour, an MBA*

## 4. Verb Tenses

### Present

| Tense | Form | Use | Example |
|-------|------|-----|---------|
| **Simple Present** | base / -s | Habits, facts, routines | She works every day. |
| **Present Continuous** | am/is/are + -ing | Actions happening now | She is working now. |
| **Present Perfect** | have/has + past participle | Past actions with present relevance | She has worked here for 5 years. |
| **Present Perfect Continuous** | have/has been + -ing | Duration up to now | She has been working all day. |

### Past

| Tense | Form | Use | Example |
|-------|------|-----|---------|
| **Simple Past** | -ed / irregular | Completed past actions | She worked yesterday. |
| **Past Continuous** | was/were + -ing | Ongoing past actions | She was working when I called. |
| **Past Perfect** | had + past participle | Action before another past action | She had worked before she left. |
| **Past Perfect Continuous** | had been + -ing | Duration before a past point | She had been working for hours. |

### Future

| Tense | Form | Use | Example |
|-------|------|-----|---------|
| **Simple Future** | will + base | Predictions, decisions | She will work tomorrow. |
| **Going to** | am/is/are going to + base | Plans, intentions | She is going to work next week. |
| **Future Continuous** | will be + -ing | Ongoing future actions | She will be working at 8. |
| **Future Perfect** | will have + past participle | Completed by a future time | She will have worked 10 years by then. |

## 5. Modal Verbs

| Modal | Use | Example |
|-------|-----|---------|
| **can** | Ability, permission | I can swim. Can I go? |
| **could** | Past ability, polite request | I could swim. Could you help? |
| **may** | Possibility, permission | It may rain. May I enter? |
| **might** | Possibility | It might rain. |
| **must** | Obligation, strong deduction | You must stop. He must be tired. |
| **have to** | Obligation | I have to work. |
| **should** | Advice, recommendation | You should study. |
| **would** | Hypothetical, polite | I would go if I could. |
| **ought to** | Advice | You ought to rest. |

---

## 6. Conditionals

| Type | Structure | Use | Example |
|------|-----------|-----|---------|
| **Zero** | If + present, present | General truths | If you heat water, it boils. |
| **First** | If + present, will + base | Real future possibility | If it rains, I will stay home. |
| **Second** | If + past, would + base | Unreal present/future | If I won the lottery, I would travel. |
| **Third** | If + past perfect, would have + pp | Unreal past | If I had studied, I would have passed. |
| **Mixed** | Combinations | Past condition → present result | If I had studied, I would be successful now. |

---

## 7. Passive Voice

**Structure:** *be* + past participle

| Tense | Active | Passive |
|-------|--------|---------|
| Present Simple | They write books. | Books are written. |
| Present Continuous | They are writing books. | Books are being written. |
| Past Simple | They wrote books. | Books were written. |
| Present Perfect | They have written books. | Books have been written. |
| Future | They will write books. | Books will be written. |
| Modal | They can write books. | Books can be written. |

## 8. Adjectives & Adverbs

### Order of Adjectives
**Opinion → Size → Age → Shape → Color → Origin → Material → Purpose**

Example: a beautiful large old round red Italian wooden dining table

### Comparative & Superlative

| Type | Comparative | Superlative |
|------|-------------|-------------|
| Short adjectives | -er | the -est |
| Long adjectives | more + adj | the most + adj |
| Irregular | good → better → best | bad → worse → worst |

### Adverbs of Frequency
always | usually | often | sometimes | rarely | never

Position: before main verb / after *be*

## 9. Prepositions

### Time
- **at**: clock times, night, weekend → *at 5 o'clock, at night*
- **on**: days, dates → *on Monday, on July 4th*
- **in**: months, years, periods → *in July, in 2024, in the morning*

### Place
- **at**: specific point → *at the door, at school*
- **on**: surface → *on the table, on the wall*
- **in**: enclosed space → *in the room, in the box*

### Common Prepositions
about, above, across, after, against, along, among, around, before, behind, below, beneath, beside, between, beyond, by, down, during, for, from, into, near, of, off, over, through, to, toward, under, until, up, with, without

## 10. Common Sentence Structures

### Basic Word Order
**Subject + Verb + Object**  
She reads books.

### Questions
- Yes/No: Auxiliary + Subject + Verb? → *Do you like coffee?*
- Wh-: Wh-word + Auxiliary + Subject + Verb? → *Where do you live?*

### Negatives
Subject + Auxiliary + not + Verb → *I do not like coffee.* / *I don't like coffee.*

### Relative Clauses
- **who** (people) → *The man who called is my friend.*
- **which** (things) → *The book which I read was great.*
- **that** (people/things) → *The car that I bought is new.*
- **whose** (possession) → *The woman whose car was stolen...*
- **where** (places) → *The city where I was born...*

## 11. Countable vs Uncountable Nouns

| Countable | Uncountable |
|-----------|-------------|
| a book, two books | water, information, advice |
| many / few / a few | much / little / a little |
| How many? | How much? |

**Note:** Some nouns can be both depending on meaning: *coffee* (uncountable) vs *a coffee* (countable).

## 12. Common Mistakes to Avoid

| Incorrect | Correct |
|-----------|---------|
| He go to school. | He goes to school. |
| She don't like it. | She doesn't like it. |
| I am agree. | I agree. |
| He is taller than me. | He is taller than I (am). *(formal)* / *than me* *(informal OK)* |
| Between you and I | Between you and me |
| I look forward to meet you. | I look forward to meeting you. |
| Despite of the rain | Despite the rain / In spite of the rain |
| She suggested me to go. | She suggested that I go. / She suggested going. |

## Quick Tips

- Always check **subject-verb agreement**.
- Use **present perfect** for experiences (*I have been to Paris*) and **simple past** for specific times (*I went to Paris in 2020*).
- Adverbs of manner usually end in **-ly** (*quickly, carefully*).
- Never put two main verbs together without a connector (*I want go* → *I want to go*).
- Practice with real sentences — context is everything!
